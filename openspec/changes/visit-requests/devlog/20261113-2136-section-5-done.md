# §5 done: the dashboard's visit form

<!-- Section: 5 · Tasks: 5.1, 5.2 ticked · Base: 60d9e68 -->

**Built:**

- **`types.ts`:**
  - `SessionState` has `waiting-on-visit`;
  - a shared `PromptKind` (`question | permission | visit`);
  - `PromptDetail.visit` (a `VisitRequest`: `url`, `reason`, `action?`);
  - `AnswerEntry.url`, and `outcome` can also be `done` or `cant`.
- **`live.ts`:** `SESSION_STATES` and `parseAnswerEntry` accept the visit state and kind. §3 left these out.
- **`promptForm.ts`:** `renderPromptForm` sends a detail with `visit` to `visitForm`. The form has, all as text:
  - the reason, the action, and the URL in full as an `<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">`, with
    " (opens in a new tab)";
  - a note `textarea` (`maxlength=1000`), and **Done** and **Can't**, which post `{ visited, message? }`.
  - `visitHref()` makes a link only for `http:`/`https:` after `new URL()`. Anything else is shown as text.
- **`sessionRows.ts`:**
  - "waiting on a visit request";
  - `NEEDING_STATES` includes the visit state;
  - `promptWhat(kind, tool)` gives "question", "permission for Bash" or "visit a page", so a teammate sees
    "Needs Emmz: visit a page";
  - `heldPrompts` gives kind `visit`, so the tab title count works unchanged;
  - a visit that isn't held shows its reason and "answer in the terminal".
- **`answerList.ts`:** for those who could answer, "Emmz visited <url> for Emmz: done" (or ": can't"). For anyone else,
  "Emmz answered a visit request for Emmz".
- **`notifications.ts`:** the body is "A visit request is waiting for you." The title stays "Needs you: <change>".
- **`styles.css`:** Done has the primary style, like Allow. The URL wraps anywhere, and the hint is muted.
