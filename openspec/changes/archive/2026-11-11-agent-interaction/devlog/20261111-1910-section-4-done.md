# §4 done: away mode, held prompts and answers from the dashboard

<!-- Section: 4 · Tasks: 4.1–4.5 ticked · Base: 917ea26 -->

All of §4 is built, self-reviewed and gated. The build is in `20261111-1905-handoff-section-4.md`. That note's
decisions and gotchas still hold. Here they are in short, so this note stands alone:

- **Who may answer (D5):** `Viewer.OwnedProjectIds`, `CanAnswer(sessionMemberId, scope)` (the session's own member,
  or an owner of the change's Project). `MembershipChanged` carries `Role`, so open streams follow a promotion or a
  demotion.
- **Redaction:** `SessionView.Prompt` (kind and tool) goes to everyone who can see the session. `PromptDetail` (id,
  held, questions, preview) goes only to those who may answer, via `SessionView.For`, `ChangeCard.SessionsFor` and
  `LiveCard.For(Viewer, ChangeScope)`, in the stream, card reads, the change page and the tag endpoint. On the web
  side, a present `promptDetail` means "Needs you".
- **Away:** `AwayMode` (scoped) at `/api/agent/away` (Reporter) and `/api/me/away` (Member). Turning it on again keeps
  the first time. Turning it off calls `ReleaseHeld`. `away-changed` goes to that member's streams only, and a
  bootstrap token gets 403.
- **Prompts:**
  - A session holds at most one prompt. It is held only when the member is away and the change has a card; otherwise
    it's `Released` from the start.
  - A pending prompt survives the session's events, and the sweep never expires its session.
  - `Settle` resets `LastEventAt`, so the stale threshold counts from the release.
  - `PromptOptions` (`Panopticon:Prompts`): 8h hold, 90s wait grace, 25s wait timeout. The sweep runs every 15s.
  - `AskAsync` re-reads `AwaySince` after holding, to guard against away going off at the same moment.
- **Answers:** in this order:
  1. Find the prompt, then check the body against its kind.
  2. Re-check in the database, in a transaction, who may answer, and insert the `RemoteAnswer` row.
  3. `TryClaim` (409 if the claim is lost), then commit; if the commit fails, `Unclaim`.
  4. `CompleteAnswer`: the session goes back to working.
  5. Publish `AnswerRecorded`.

  Claimed prompts are skipped by both the release and the sweep.
- **Answer list:** `ChangePage.Answers` holds up to 200, newest first, with the questions only for viewers who could
  have answered.
