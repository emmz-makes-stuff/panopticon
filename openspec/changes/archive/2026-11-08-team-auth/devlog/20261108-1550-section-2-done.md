## What landed

- **2.1 Email.**
  - `Resend` 0.24.0 is pinned in `Directory.Packages.props`. Its API is `IResend.EmailSendAsync(EmailMessage, ct)`,
    registered with `AddResend(o => { o.ApiToken; o.ThrowExceptions = true; })`.
  - `Auth/Email.cs` has `IEmailSender`, `OutgoingEmail(To, Subject, Text, Html)`, `EmailOptions`,
    `ResendEmailSender`, and `LoggingEmailSender`. The logging sender writes the whole email at Warning.
  - `ServerSetup` picks `LoggingEmailSender` only in Development with no `Panopticon:Resend:ApiKey`.
  - Outside Development, start-up needs `Resend:ApiKey`, `Email:From` and `PublicUrl`. A blank `PublicUrl`
    counts as unset. `Email:From` is needed whenever a key is set, Development included.
- **2.2 `POST /api/auth/sign-in`** answers 202 with `{message: "Check your email…"}` for any valid address. It's
  a 400 for an invalid one, and a 429 over a limit.
  - **Emails go through `EmailOutbox`**, a `BackgroundService` reading a bounded channel. Sending in the
    background keeps the response time the same for members and strangers, a timing oracle the spec's "same
    response" implies.
    - A failed send is logged with its subject only, never the link, and the outbox keeps going.
    - The class is `EmailOutbox` rather than `EmailQueue` because of CA1711.
  - `AuthLimits` is a singleton with in-memory sliding windows: sign-in 5 per 15 minutes per email key and 20 per
    15 minutes per client IP; device start 10 per 15 minutes per IP; approvals 10 per minute per session. The
    device and approval limits are ready for §5. Limits apply to strangers' addresses too, so they reveal
    nothing.
  - Links are built on `PublicUrl` (`…/sign-in/confirm#<secret>`). Only Development falls back to the request's
    host.
  - The email text is in `SignInEmail`, which settles design's open question about the copy.
- **2.3 `POST /api/auth/confirm {secret}`** answers 200 with `SessionMember {id, email, displayName}`.
  - A refusal is a 400 problem with an `extensions.reason` of `invalid`, `used` or `expired`. The confirm page
    in 8.2 will word its message from that.
  - Spending is atomic: `ExecuteUpdate … WHERE used_at IS NULL`.
  - `Auth/Sessions.cs` has `SessionStore` (start, end, resolve and slide at most once a minute, and `IsLiveAsync`
    for §3.2's SSE re-check) and `SessionHandler` (scheme `Session`).
  - The cookie is `panopticon_session`: HttpOnly, Lax, Secure outside Development, path `/`, and its `Expires`
    is re-issued on each slide.
- **2.4 `GET /api/session` and `POST /api/auth/sign-out`** both need the `Member` policy.
  - **Deviation from D6, stricter on purpose:** `RequestHeaderMiddleware` refuses *every* non-GET `/api` request
    without `X-Panopticon-Request: 1` (a 403 problem). That includes sign-in, confirm, and the §5 device
    endpoints, so **the CLI's login (7.3) must send the header.** Future endpoints can't forget it.
- **Auth registration** now has no default scheme.
  - Policy `AuthPolicies.Member` uses the Session scheme and needs the session claim. Policy
    `AuthPolicies.Reporter` uses the Bootstrap scheme for now; 6.1 changes it to ApiToken.
  - `/mcp` uses `Reporter`. Claims live in `PanopticonClaims` (`MemberId` = NameIdentifier, `SessionId`).

## Test infrastructure, for later sections

- `PanopticonFactory` gains:
  - `Emails` (`CapturingEmailSender`, on by default; `CaptureEmail = false` uses the real registration);
  - `Logs` (`LogCapture`, Information and up);
  - `Clock` (an optional `TimeProvider`, such as `ManualClock`);
  - default settings for `PublicUrl` (`http://panopticon.test/`), a fake Resend key and `From`;
  - `CreateBrowserClient()` (https, keeps cookies, so `Secure` works under Testing);
  - `CreateSignedInClientAsync(email)`, a real sign-in through a captured link.
- `ApiRequests.PostApiAsync` and `SendApiAsync` add the D6 header. `CapturingEmailSender.WaitForAsync(address,
  count)` relies on the outbox's FIFO order: once a later email has arrived, an earlier one would have too.
