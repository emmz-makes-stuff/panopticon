## Self-review fixes

- **Startup with the server unreachable.** If `GET /api/session` failed with anything other than a 401,
  `startShell` threw and `main.ts` dropped the rejection with `void`, which left a blank page. It now shows
  "Couldn't reach the server" (`role="alert"`) in the view, starts no router, and returns `router: null`.
  `Shell.router` is therefore `Router | null`.
- **Sign out failing silently.** A failure other than a 401 was swallowed. It now shows "Couldn't sign out.
  Try again." in `.account-error` next to the button, and you stay signed in.
- Both have Vitest tests in `shell.test.ts`.
- Dropped an `aria-current` style, because nothing sets that attribute.
