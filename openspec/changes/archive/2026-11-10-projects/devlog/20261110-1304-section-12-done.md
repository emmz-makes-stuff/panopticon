**The decisions:**
- **12.4, the lock order:** Projects before members, everywhere.
  - **Removal from the server** now locks every Project the member belongs to, then the active members with
    `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` (not `FOR UPDATE`, so the foreign keys' `KEY SHARE` checks from reports and invites never
    wait on it), then re-reads their memberships.
  - **A membership in an unlocked Project** (committed just before the member lock) gives a 409, "They joined a
    Project while you were removing them. Try again." A Project can't be locked after a member without breaking the
    order.
- **12.5, the member-row lock:** an invite (`MemberInvites.FindOrAddAsync`) and **creating a Project** both lock the
  member's row with `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` first. Creating a Project wasn't in the review: a removed member could still
  get a Project from a create that was racing the removal. It now answers 403, "You've been removed from the server."
- **The tests:** `MembershipLockingTests` (4 tests) hold locks from a raw `NpgsqlConnection` and poll
  `pg_stat_activity` for a lock wait. **All four failed on the old code** (I checked with a stash) and pass now.
- **12.6, the tidy-ups:**
  - `MemberProjects.Of(db, memberId)` is the one query, used by the Projects page and `list_projects`.
  - `RecordedReport.Retired` is a `RetiredCopy(ChangeId, Scope)`.
  - The web renders the role-change PATCH's `ProjectDetails`, with no second GET. Two fake servers in
    `projects.test.ts` were answering with a member, matching the client's old type.
- **12.3:** `CardProject.Of(Project)`. `HistoryChoicesAsync` keeps its own `new CardProject(...)` from a SQL
  projection.
