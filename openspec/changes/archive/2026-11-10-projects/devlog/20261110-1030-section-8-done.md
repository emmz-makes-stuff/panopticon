  - **`web/src/projectLabel.ts`:** `codePlate` moved here from `views/projects.ts`, so `card.ts` doesn't import a
    view.
    - **`projectLabel`:** the code (with the name as its `title`), "Personal", or null for a bootstrap change
      (`project: null, personal: false`).
    - **`leadingProjectLabel`:** the plate and its space, or nothing.
  - **Where it goes:** it leads the card's host line ("PAN github.com"), the change page's repo line, and the history
    row's meta line.
  - **The types:** `CardProject`, plus `project` and `personal` on `ChangeCard` and `HistoryEntry`.
  - **The fixtures:** `card()` and the history `entry()` default to `project: null, personal: false`, so no label
    shows and older tests' text stays as it was.
- **8.2, the `project` filter:**
  - **Parsing:** `Filters.project` is a lower-case id or `PERSONAL` (`"personal"`). Anything else is dropped.
  - **The key order:** `project` comes first in `FILTER_KEYS`, `HISTORY_KEYS` and the bar's dimensions ("Project",
    "All Projects"). `app.ts` now passes `FILTER_KEYS` rather than its own list.
  - **Matching:** by id. Personal means `personal && project === null`.
  - **The choices:** `filterChoices` (the dashboard) collects the Projects on the cards, named as on the newest card,
    and `personal`. `FilterChoices` gained `projects` and `personal`, which is the server's `HistoryChoices` shape,
    so the history and metrics get them from `/api/history/filters`.
  - **The options:** "PAN: Panopticon" by code, then "Personal". A missing id shows "Unknown Project (no
    changes)", and a missing personal shows "Personal (no changes)".
  - **The server:** its metrics endpoint already parses `project` (`MetricsEndpoints.cs:36`).
- **8.3, retired copies:** `HistoryEntry.archiveFolder` is `string | null`, and it gained `linkedTo`. A retired copy's
  row says "Linked to PAN on 8 Nov 2026" in place of "Archived 8 Nov 2026", after its "Personal" plate.
- **`DESIGN.md`:** where the small plate sits, and a Project-filter bullet in "Filters and quiet cards".
