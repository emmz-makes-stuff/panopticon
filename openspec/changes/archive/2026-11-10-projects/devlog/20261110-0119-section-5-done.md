**What landed:**
- **5.0, retired copies:**
  - **The schema:** `Change.RetiredToProjectId`, with its `RetiredToProject` navigation, a foreign key to projects and
    an index. The migration `20261110011205_RetiredCopies` also adds the check `ck_changes_retired_unfoldered` (a
    retired copy is archived and has no folder). There's no backfill, since this branch hasn't shipped.
  - **The recorder** sets the column when D4 retires a personal change.
  - **The metrics:** `MetricsReader.ForTeamAsync` leaves out `RetiredToProjectId != null`. `ForChangeAsync` still
    answers for a retired change.
  - **The history:** `HistoryEntry.ArchiveFolder` is now `string?`, and there's a new `LinkedTo` (a `CardProject`).
  - **Tests:** `Changes/RetiredCopyTests`, plus an assertion in `ReportScopeTests`.
- **5.1, members (D8):**
  - **`Auth/InitialMemberList`:** a singleton that reads `Panopticon:InitialMembers` on each call, through
    `InitialMembers.Read`.
  - **`GET /api/members`:** active members who share a Project with you, and you.
  - **`POST /api/members`:** removed, with its `InviteAsync` and `InviteRequest`. It now answers 405.
  - **`DELETE /api/members/{id}`:**
    - **Initial members only:** anyone else gets a 403, "Only an initial member can remove members from the server."
    - **The last-owner rule:** inside the transaction, it locks the projects the target owns (`FOR UPDATE`, in id
      order, as the Project endpoints lock projects). If the target is the only owner of any, it answers 409, "They're
      the only owner of ACME, ZED. Make another member an owner there first."
    - **On success:** it also deletes their `project_members` rows.
  - **`SessionMember`:** gains `CanRemoveMembers`, on `GET /api/session` and in the sign-in confirm response.
  - **Tests:** `MemberEndpointTests` rewritten. Invites go through Projects. The two-removers race test configures both
    addresses as initial members. `TokenEndpointTests` and `ApiTokenReportTests` now invite through a Project.
