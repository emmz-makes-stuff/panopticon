**5.1, the `frontend-design` pass, within `web/DESIGN.md`.** The new "Metrics" section of `DESIGN.md` is the record.
The choices:
- **The one bold thing: the duration rule.** A 3px `--ink-muted` line, as long as its time against the longest
  beside it, at least 2% (`--share`, set by `durationRule()` in `metricsPanel.ts`). It's dashed while running and
  `aria-hidden`.
  - **Why:** the proposal's why is that a slow section stands out after the fact, the way a quiet card does live.
  - **Not teal:** time isn't progress, so the rule is never teal. Teal stays for the chart's bars, as D5 asks.
- **Rejected:** stat tiles with a big-number hero, the generic dashboard default. The figures are a plain `dl` row
  between hairlines instead, with Gabarito 25px values and two to a row on a phone.
- **The middot, decided:** "in progress · 1h so far" (from D5) becomes "in progress, 1h so far", per DESIGN.md
  principle 5. `metricsPanel.ts` and its test are updated to match.
- **The panel:** a grid of number, title, rule, then time. On a phone (≤40rem), `grid-auto-flow: row dense` drops
  the rule under the title. `order` can't do that with `display: contents` rows.
- **Slowest rows:** reordered to the change link first (what the row links to), then "Section N: title", then the
  time on the right, then a full-width rule. The test expectations follow.
- **The chart:** `.chart-svg` is 32–40rem wide and scrolls sideways on a phone, rather than shrinking its 6-unit
  text below reading size.
- **The page:** `.metrics-page` (44rem) is added in `views/metrics.ts`, since `page()` only sets `page`.
- **Tests:** `metricsPanel.test.ts` (2) covers the rule's shares and the duration's forms.
- **Not seen yet:** no screenshot was taken this session. How the panel and the page actually look (desktop and
  phone, light and dark) is part of the 6.2 human check.
